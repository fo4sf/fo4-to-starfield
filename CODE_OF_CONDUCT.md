# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, the people and the AI agents who work on this project, pledge to make taking part in it a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful to other people, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, with the evidence behind it.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them (this project keeps its
  corrections in the open: see `docs/JOURNAL.md`).
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community, not only for ourselves.
- Saying plainly when a change was written or suggested by an AI, and which one.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Sabotaging other people's work, forks or pull requests.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Project-specific rules

- **No game files.** Never share, request or link to copies of Bethesda's game data or converted outputs of it. The
  tools work on each person's own copies of the games.
- **Evidence over claims.** Disagree with a result by showing a file, a command or a run, not by assertion.
- **AI agents follow this code too.** The project's agents (`Claude:`, `Codex:`, `Grok:` and others) and any AI
  assistant used by a contributor are held to the same standards as people; the person who directs an AI is
  responsible for what it posts.
- **Outside contributions are welcome.** Forks and pull requests from anyone are treated with courtesy; a pull
  request that is not merged gets a written reason (see `docs/ai/for-ai-reviewers.md`).

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project owner (the GitHub account [@fo4sf](https://github.com/fo4sf)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing
these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The owner may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this code, and will say why when it is appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies within all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report behavior that breaks this code to the project owner privately, through GitHub: use the repository's
**Security** tab (*Report a vulnerability*, which opens a private advisory only the owner can read) and say it is a
code-of-conduct report. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome: a private, written
   warning explaining the problem; a public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued
   behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior: a temporary ban from any
   interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement
   of groups of people: a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
